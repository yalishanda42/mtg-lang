# Bulgarian probe of the plan IR

Generated by `scripts/bg_probe.py` from `docs/bg-probe.yaml`. Each Bulgarian rendering was written from
the clause plan (`scripts/plan_dump.py`) and the glossary's `bg` labels alone, to check that the
language-neutral planner carries everything a Bulgarian realizer needs (English renderer plan, Task 11).

**Result:** three facts were missing and are now in the plan — `ClausePlan.aspect` (state *има* /
change *придобива*), `ClausePlan.tense = past` for completed events in conditions (*са загинали*), and
`parts.once` for triggers on one-time events (*когато встъпи* / *щом напада*). Everything else the
Bulgarian needed is either in the plan or a property of a word (gender, numeral and participle forms) —
data for a future `lex.bg`, not for the planner.
